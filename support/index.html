Ayako

Support

Ayako is a basketball training log for iPhone: workouts, drills, makes and shots, and video of your reps you can slow down, compare side by side and take notes on.

Contact

For questions, problems or ideas, open an issue at github.com/rbuckets/rbuckets.github.io/issues. Please include your iPhone model and iOS version if something isn't working.

Common questions

Where is my data? Only on your iPhone. There are no accounts and nothing is uploaded. See the Privacy Policy.

Can I move my workouts to a new phone? Yes, through an iPhone backup (iCloud or a computer) restored to the new phone.

A shared workout link opens in Safari instead of Ayako. Press and hold the link and choose to open it in Ayako, or make sure Ayako is installed.
