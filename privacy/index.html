Ayako

Privacy Policy

Last updated October 21, 2026

Ayako is a basketball training log. Everything you put in it stays on your iPhone. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and we don't collect, sell or share any data about you.

What Ayako stores, and where

  • Your workouts: drills, tags, notes, makes and shots, and the clips you record or import. These are kept in the app on your phone only.
  • Your answers from setup: your name, age group, years playing and why you use Ayako. These are kept on your phone only and are used to personalize the app (for example, "Welcome back").

Deleting the app deletes all of this. If your iPhone backs up to iCloud or a computer, the backup includes Ayako's data, as it does for other apps; that is handled by Apple, not by us.

Camera, microphone and photos

Ayako asks for the camera and microphone only to record drill videos, and for your photo library only when you import a video or save one. Videos stay in the app on your phone unless you choose to share or save them.

Sharing a workout

When you share a workout, Ayako makes a link that contains the workout's name, date, and drill names and tags, and nothing else (no notes, clips or stats). That information is in the part of the link after the "#", which browsers never send to a web server, so it isn't sent to or stored by us. Whoever you send the link to can read it.

Children

Ayako can be used by players of any age, including middle schoolers. Because it collects no personal information, it collects none from children either.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Questions: see Support.
